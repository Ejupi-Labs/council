# Quickstart: Scoped Retention and Recovery

**Draft:** These are acceptance journeys, not commands that exist today. Payload eligibility and archive intake remain open design gates. Do not use a real provider or vendor credentials for development verification.

1. With a controlled owner and two populated project fixtures, preview a scoped evidence export. Verify the selected eligible inventory, explicit project-file and artifact-payload dispositions, completeness, destination and unrelated-project exclusion before creating anything.
2. With a qualifying production-use project under the ultimately approved eligibility policy, create and verify a complete scoped backup. Inspect its manifest and restore it into a new absent isolated destination. Confirm required records, relationships and eligible content are available in Council; no existing project changes and no task or writer resumes. A synthetic-only or empty-project round trip does not establish this gate.
3. For a project containing inaccessible, unknown or ineligible required material, verify that the operation reports exact exclusions and never claims a complete backup. Separately export and import an explicitly incomplete metadata history and confirm it cannot dispatch or authorize effects.
4. Preview cleanup for one task among two. Authorize a specific unchanged item, alter another after review, and leave unrelated and shared items. Confirm only the authorized still-eligible item is disposed, all other work survives, and every result and retained audit is visible.
5. Lose a response and retry the identical captured operation from the other interface; restart the owner at each durable effect boundary. Verify no duplicated publication, restore, deletion or model admission, and require explicit owner-mediated reconciliation wherever effects remain unknown.
6. Reject corrupt, incompatible, duplicate, oversized, traversal, link, collision and changed-destination cases before effects. Exercise anchored identity and swap races on each supported platform with fake fixtures only.
7. Exercise the actual UI at 360 pixels and by keyboard, preserving drafts and selections across stale responses. Verify UI and CLI show the same operation ID, manifest, outcome and unresolved incident.

Final acceptance requires independent review, applicable corrections, `npm run build`, `npm test`, `npm run check` and the six native plus race CI matrix on the exact source. Record controlled fake versus unperformed live scope honestly.
